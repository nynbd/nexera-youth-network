Our Mission

Young people don't need to wait for permission to lead.

NextEra Youth Network exists because good ideas from young people too often stay ideas — no mentorship, no funding path, no room to try. We close that gap with structured training, hands-on projects, and a network of peers and mentors who back each other's work.

Every program we run ends with something real: a project shipped, a skill practiced in public, a connection made. That's the standard we hold ourselves to.
